Quick Start

1. Include Styles

Include spinner.css in your page head:

<link rel="stylesheet" href="spinner.css">

2. Embed SVG Snippet

Copy snippet-o1.html or snippet-o2.html into your HTML markup:

<!-- Option 1 -->
<div class="gl-spinner gl-spinner--o1" data-theme="dark" style="--gl-size:48px"></div>

<!-- Option 2 -->
<div class="gl-spinner gl-spinner--o2" data-theme="dark" style="--gl-size:48px" role="status" aria-label="Loading"></div>
